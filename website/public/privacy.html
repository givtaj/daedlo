Privacy & contact

Updated 9 October 2026

This notice covers the public Daedlo website, software downloads, and correspondence about them. The website does not run checks against your application.

Who operates Daedlo

Daedlo is operated by Mohammad Reza Givtaj, a self-employed individual based in Spain and the controller responsible for the personal data described here.

For general and product enquiries, contact info@daedlo.com. For support and privacy requests, contact support@daedlo.com.

Browsing this site

The site has no account registration, contact form, file uploads, or payment collection. The workflow illustration runs in your browser and does not send your selections to us. The site’s own code does not use cookies, local storage, analytics, or advertising trackers.

Cloudflare provides website and download delivery, and security. Serving pages and files involves technical request information, such as IP addresses and network data. Provider handling and retention are described in Cloudflare’s privacy policy. This is separate from the interactive illustration, which makes no network requests.

If you contact us

We receive your sender address and the contents you choose to send. Contact email uses Cloudflare Email Routing and a Google mailbox. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, or sensitive source material.

We use correspondence to answer your enquiry and address your request, based on our legitimate interests in providing support. Where a legal obligation applies, including handling privacy-rights requests, we process the information to meet that obligation. Correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations.

Providers and international processing

Cloudflare and Google process information while providing hosting or email services. Processing may take place outside the European Economic Area. Their published privacy information and applicable processing terms describe their safeguards: Cloudflare privacy, Cloudflare processing terms, and Google privacy.

Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your personal information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact support@daedlo.com and identify your request as relating to Daedlo.

You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your relevant supervisory authority. We may need proportionate information to verify a request before disclosing or changing personal data.

Product access and future changes

Software licensing is described in the licence texts and notices included with each download. See the installation page for the public preview kit. This site does not offer hosted QA execution. Any future account or paid-service offering will need its own applicable terms and an updated explanation of its data processing.
