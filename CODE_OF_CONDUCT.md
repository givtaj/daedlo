# Daedlo code of conduct

Daedlo welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience and ways of working.
Help others participate by explaining decisions, giving specific feedback and
allowing room for questions and mistakes. Critique the work with care for the
person doing it.

In project issues, pull requests, discussions and project-organized spaces:

- Treat people respectfully, including when disagreeing.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or target another person.
- Do not use sexualized attention, personal attacks or repeated unwanted contact.
- Respect privacy; do not share someone else's personal information or private
  messages without permission.
- Follow reasonable moderation requests and keep collaboration focused on the
  project.

Report a concern privately to [support@daedlo.com](mailto:support@daedlo.com) with
the subject “Daedlo conduct report”. Describe what happened, where it happened and
any relevant evidence you can safely share. The current project maintainer handles
reports; this small project does not have an independent moderation team.

Maintainers consider the context and impact, may request clarification, and may
remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or ban an account.
Reports are handled privately, with information shared only as needed to assess
and respond. No absolute confidentiality or response-time guarantee is promised.
A participant can request reconsideration through the same contact, explaining
what they believe was missed.
